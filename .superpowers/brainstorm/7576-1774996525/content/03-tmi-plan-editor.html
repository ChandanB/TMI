Key Differences from Student Profile

Student Profile
  • Interests/Careers/Resources belong to the student
  • Career section shows AI recommendations
  • "Explore Careers" opens full explorer sheet
  • No forms or approval sections
  • Data persists on the student record
TMI Plan Editor
  • Interests/Careers/Resources are linked to the plan
  • Can pull from selected students' existing data
  • Includes Forms & Surveys management
  • Includes Goals, Progress tracking
  • Has Approval workflow section
